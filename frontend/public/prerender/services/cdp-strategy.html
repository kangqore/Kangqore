Customer Data Strategy

Architect a robust first-party data ecosystem that connects every touchpoint, ensuring data sovereignty and highly personalized customer experiences.

Build a "single source of truth" for customer data to combat the death of cookies.

The customer data planes, for governed, unified identity.

Connecting fragmented touchpoints into a unified customer data engine with real-time event streaming, identity resolution, privacy compliance, and reverse ETL activation.

  • Event Tracking & Ingestion: Cross-platform event streaming across web, mobile, and server SDKs with automated schema validation. Managed: Twilio Segment,RudderStack Cloud,mParticle. Self-hosted: RudderStack Open Source,Snowplow event pipelines on AWS/GCP.
  • Identity Resolution & Profiles: Deterministic and probabilistic identity graph stitching across anonymous visitor IDs and user emails. Managed: Simon Data,Treasure Data,Amperity. Self-hosted: Graph databases (Neo4j),dbt identity resolution models on Snowflake/BigQuery.
  • Customer Lakehouse & Warehouse: Centralized repository storing full customer event histories, behavioral metrics, and RFM segments. Managed: Snowflake Data Cloud,Google BigQuery,Databricks. Self-hosted: ClickHouse,PostgreSQL analytics clusters,Apache Iceberg tables.
  • Reverse ETL & Audience Activation: Synchronizing warehouse-computed cohorts directly into CRM, ad platforms, and marketing automation tools. Managed: Census,Hightouch,Octolis. Self-hosted: Custom dbt reverse ETL scripts,Apache Airflow sync pipelines.
  • Privacy, Consent & Governance: Enforcing user consent preferences, cookie policies, right-to-be-forgotten deletion workflows, and audit trails. Managed: OneTrust,Ketch,Transcend Privacy. Self-hosted: Custom consent management daemons,GDPR/CCPA automated deletion workers.
  • Behavioral Segmentation & AI: Predictive churn scoring, customer lifetime value modeling, and automated micro-segmentation. Managed: Pecan AI,Faraday,Amplitude Cohorts. Self-hosted: Scikit-learn churn models,Python lifetime value (LTV) regression pipelines.
