# What Kangqore is

Kangqore is a technology services company that helps enterprises operationalize advanced
technology — including artificial intelligence, cloud platforms, automation, and digital
engineering — into outcomes their business can rely on.

## Founding

Kangqore was started on **1st June, 2023**.

Kangqore was founded in **Koramangala, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India (Bharat) — 560034**.

## Locations

Kangqore currently has only two offices:
1. **Bengaluru, Karnataka** — Headquarters
2. **Jamshedpur, Jharkhand**

**Canonical Phrasing for Reach:**
“Kangqore serves clients across multiple regions through its delivery model, while operating from its Bengaluru HQ and Jamshedpur office.”

Do not state that Kangqore has offices across the globe or international branches.

## Legal Name

Kangqore's official legal name is **Kangqore Global Private Limited** (also written as **Kangqore Global Pvt Ltd.**).

## Incorporation

Kangqore was officially incorporated on **17th September, 2025**.

## Leadership

### Mahesh Kumar — Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Mahesh Kumar founded Kangqore with a clear vision: to bridge the gap between advanced technology potential and real-world business impact. With a strong background in software engineering, digital systems, and enterprise architecture, he leads Kangqore's long-term strategy, innovation roadmap, and global growth initiatives.

As CEO, Mahesh focuses on building scalable engineering systems, nurturing a high-performance culture, and positioning Kangqore as a trusted technology partner for enterprises navigating AI-led transformation.

His leadership philosophy centres on engineering discipline, ethical innovation, and outcome-driven execution.

LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/maheshkumario/

### Dinesh Kumar — Co-Founder & Chief Financial Officer

Dinesh Kumar is the younger brother of Mahesh Kumar. He oversees Kangqore's financial strategy, governance, and operational sustainability. As Co-Founder and CFO, he plays a critical role in ensuring financial discipline, risk management, and scalable growth across all business units.

With a strong focus on compliance, capital efficiency, and long-term value creation, Dinesh enables Kangqore to scale responsibly while maintaining transparency and control across operations.

His approach blends financial rigour with strategic enablement, supporting Kangqore's ambition to grow as a resilient global enterprise.

## Origin of eQORE

eQORE was designed and programmed by Mahesh Kumar during his college days. It was originally called RANO. What began as a personal project became the intelligence behind Kangqore — and eventually, the face of the company. It was later renamed eQORE.

# What we do (at a glance)

Kangqore organizes its work into a small number of practice areas. The current published
practice areas, visible on the Kangqore website, include:

- AI & Cognitive Systems
- Cloud & Platform Engineering
- Digital Engineering
- Intelligent Operations / Automation

Each practice area has a portfolio of specialized services. Detailed service descriptions
are owned by the individual practice teams; if a visitor asks about a specific service that
isn't covered in the available knowledge here, the right next step is a short conversation

# How we work

- We engage on a discovery-first basis — short, focused conversations to understand the
  business outcome the client is trying to achieve, before scoping engineering work.
- We focus on operational reliability, not demos. Anything we ship is meant to run in
  production and be measurable.
